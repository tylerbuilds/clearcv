# ClearCV brand kit

ClearCV is a free, open-source CV builder. Its original mark is a solid C with a clean, angled opening, paired with a serif wordmark. Terracotta and warm paper match the editor.

## Download the artwork

- [Terracotta mark](../public/brand/clearcv-mark.svg)
- [Single-colour mark](../public/brand/clearcv-mark-mono.svg)
- [Wordmark for light backgrounds](../public/brand/clearcv-wordmark.svg)
- [Wordmark for dark backgrounds](../public/brand/clearcv-wordmark-light.svg)
- [Browser icon](../public/brand/favicon.svg)

The SVGs are small, editable and have transparent backgrounds. They are original ClearCV artwork, included under this repository's [MIT licence](../LICENSE). Each SVG keeps the copyright and licence notice in a comment. They contain no external images, scripts or bundled fonts.

## Use the identity

Write the name **ClearCV**, with both C and V capitalised. Give the mark clear space of at least a quarter of its width, and use it at 16 pixels or larger. Keep its proportions and angled opening intact. The single-colour SVG uses `currentColor` when embedded inline and defaults to black when opened as an image.

The wordmark uses Georgia with Times New Roman and the browser's serif fallback. These fonts are requested from the viewer's device; no font files are redistributed. Use the mark by itself where an exact typeface or a very small size is needed.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terracotta | `#a94f2e` | Mark and key actions |
| Ink | `#292c30` | Wordmark and headings |
| Warm paper | `#fffdf9` | Light surfaces |
| Pale clay | `#f4dfcc` | Mark on dark surfaces |

For a project link, use [ClearCV on GitHub](https://github.com/tylerbuilds/clearcv) or the [live editor](https://tylerbuilds.github.io/clearcv/). The app's **Open source** and **MIT licence** links point directly to its public source and licence.
