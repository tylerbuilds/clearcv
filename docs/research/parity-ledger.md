# Free and premium feature ledger

Research dates: 9–10 October 2026. Scope includes Enhancv's free tools, free access, Pro features and the separately documented Business features. Premium capabilities are part of the research scope. ClearCV does not put implemented capabilities behind a subscription.

The evidence below comes from anonymous official product and Help Centre pages. It establishes what Enhancv advertises, rather than proving its implementation, effectiveness or authenticated editor behaviour. No private accounts, paid access, proprietary code or target templates were used. See [REA provenance](rea-provenance.md) for the separate browser investigation.

“Baseline implemented” refers to the initial ClearCV workflow recorded in [verification](../verification.md). “Coordinator confirms implementation” identifies the current expansion under integration; final browser, print and Linux/macOS receipts must confirm release readiness. “Module verified” identifies executable pure behaviour checked by `tests/review.test.js`. The expansion interface is integrated; independent browser and platform receipts remain the release gate. “Remaining” is a known capability gap, and “unknown” is a research limit. These states do not establish complete parity.

## Official plan evidence

The [free-plan Help Centre article](https://help.enhancv.com/en/articles/1208865-what-is-included-in-the-free-version), updated 5 February 2026, describes seven days of free access, all templates and design tools, basic sections, branding and a maximum of 12 section items. It lists Pro as supporting 300 resumes and cover letters, real-time content suggestions, an ATS check, Pro sections, removal of branding, unlimited section items and additional design options. The article also says users can try most Pro features during free access. This is not evidence of an unlimited permanent free editor.

The [pricing page](https://enhancv.com/pricing/) confirms those feature families, but its anonymous text extraction returned `NaN` and zero values for the paid price. Exact paid amounts are **unknown** from this observation. No price is inferred from broken markup, a blog or a different region.

Individual tools have their own availability statements: the [cover-letter generator](https://enhancv.com/cover-letter-generator/) says its generator is free without a login; the [job tracker](https://enhancv.com/features/job-application-tracker/) says the tracker is free with an account. A free tool statement does not establish free access to every feature in the subscribed editor.

## Document upgrade contract

The current core workflow adds a reviewed document upgrade without changing the CV or workspace schema. Extraction never edits a saved CV. The user compares the extracted source and editable mappings, then creates a new CV or selects fields and section merge modes for an independent upgraded copy. Existing CVs, snapshots, the master, applications and submitted attachments remain unchanged. Case and whitespace differences are preserved when skipping exact duplicate entries. Ambiguous or over-limit facts remain in the retained source and are disclosed for manual review.

Local document workers, fonts, dictionaries and OCR language data are packaged assets. There are no automatic provider calls. PDF/image limits are 10 MB, 20 PDF pages and 32 million source image pixels; other documents are limited to 2 MB and all extracted text to 50,000 characters. Referenced DOCX headers, footers, notes and text boxes are read with inert hyperlink targets; unsupported legacy Word parts are explicitly disclosed. Passwords remain in memory. Cancellation invalidates older extraction responses. The [document upgrade guide](../document-upgrades.md) states the exact supported formats, source preservation and spelling limits. Successful model tests establish those contracts; current browser, OCR, layout and opened-export receipts establish the corresponding integration separately.

## Tracker expansion contract

The tracker wave is independently written from official public capability descriptions checked on 10 October 2026, plus the previous source comparison. It adds optional `archived`, `tasks`, `customFields` and `submissions` fields to application records in the existing versioned workspace. Missing fields receive additive defaults; malformed supplied fields reject the whole import. Earlier CV and workspace fields are preserved. Saved unreadable workspace recovery and the original legacy browser value retain their previous protections. The active CV document schema and optional cloud payload do not change; applications remain local.

Submitted copies hold complete detached CV and letter state. There is no ordinary edit/replace operation for an attachment; deleting a copy is explicit, and opening it for editing creates an independent library document. `submittedAt` is a canonical UTC capture timestamp, displayed locally; applied/due dates are supplied `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar dates. Analytics receive the browser's local today explicitly, compare those dates without inventing event history, exclude archived rows and count today minus six days through today inclusively. Completed task dates do not remain pending; a legacy follow-up date remains pending until cleared. Limits are 150 application records, 30 tasks, 20 custom fields and 8 copies per record, within the unchanged shared 4 MB workspace bound.

`src/tracker.js` publishes strict validation, `filterApplications`, `applicationAnalytics` and `applicationsToCsv`. CSV quotes all cells and protects formula/control prefixes with an apostrophe. It includes exact record text, custom-label columns, task summaries and submitted-copy metadata, excluding submitted document bodies. It is not a full workspace backup or spreadsheet import path. Custom labels are case-sensitive; repeated labels in one record join values with line breaks. All model and helper tests pass under the qualified Node runtime; expanded browser, print and platform verification must be recorded separately before release.

## Local review and tailoring contract

`src/review.js` publishes `REVIEW_RULES`, `reviewCV(doc)`, `tailoringSuggestions(doc, jobAd = doc.jobAd)` and `coverLetterDraft(doc, company = '', role = '')`. They do not mutate input or make external calls.

- `reviewCV` returns `method`, `issues`, `metrics` and detached `rules`. Each issue has `ruleId`, `severity` (`attention` or `suggestion`), `path`, `message`, `suggestion` and a supplied-text `evidence` excerpt. Paths identify a document property; `description:2` means its second original text line. Hidden content sections are excluded from review and word counts. Profile fields remain essential. No aggregate score is returned.
- Writing thresholds: fewer than 15 or more than 90 summary words; more than 40 words in one description line; more than eight lines for one experience; fewer than 100 or more than 1,000 visible CV words. These are editing prompts, not page limits or suitability judgements.
- The phrase lists are deliberately small and visible: summary phrases `hard-working`, `results-oriented`/`results-orientated`, `team player`, `go-getter`, `self-motivated`, `dynamic professional`; responsibility openings `responsible for`, `duties included`, `worked on`, `helped`/`helped with`, `assisted with`. Case, spaces and supported hyphens are normalised where documented in the source.
- Numerical evidence means only that a supplied experience description contains a digit. This cannot establish achievement or accuracy; qualitative evidence remains valid. Missing digits produce a conditional prompt to use verifiable detail, never an invented metric.
- Dates accept years, English month/year or `YYYY-MM`, a single completion date or a range ending in a date, `Present`, `Current` or `Now`. Unrecognised formats require human review. Consistency checks do not invent months or require chronological ordering. No system clock or career-gap judgement is used.
- `tailoringSuggestions` returns `method`, `keywords`, `found`, `missing`, `experienceOrder`, `skillOrder`, `suggestions` and `rankings`. The order arrays contain existing IDs only. Each ranking gives `id` and `matchedKeywords`; each suggestion gives `path`, `message` and `matchedKeywords`. It compares all stored text, including custom/hidden content, using up to 24 frequent job tokens, ClearCV's common-word filter and exact token equality. Distinct overlaps determine order and tied entries stay in their original order. No stemming, semantic comparison or hiring score is used.
- `coverLetterDraft` returns `method`, editable full `text`, structured `body`, `salutation`, `closing`, `warnings` and `usedPaths`. It reuses the supplied summary, first non-empty experience (or education), up to six supplied skill names and the signature. The opening uses the supplied role or headline and company if available. Missing facts are omitted and warned about; no placeholders, inferred motivation, fabricated outcomes or job-ad assertions are inserted.

`node --test tests/review.test.js` passed 17 checks during module handoff on Linux, covering date formats, repeated/long lines, hidden/custom content, contact details, stable exact-token ranking, non-mutation, rule detachment and factual draft assembly. This module receipt does not establish UI, PDF or macOS qualification of the expanded release.

## Evidence still required for complete parity

The free and premium research scope is broader than the implemented release. Authenticated workflows, exact plan gates by region, the complete template/section catalogue, parser compatibility, AI correctness and remote service behaviour remain unknown or incomplete. Module tests cannot prove them. A release must retain the remaining rows and attach real integration receipts rather than marking a family complete because a basic local analogue exists.
